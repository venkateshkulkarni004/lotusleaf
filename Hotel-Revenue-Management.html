Hotel revenue management

Make informed pricing and demand decisions

Insight-led pricing, forecasting, and optimization help hospitality teams make disciplined commercial decisions. The right approach brings booking pace, demand signals, distribution, and the property's goals into regular review.

Discuss revenue priorities

Revenue management for hotels and resorts

Revenue management is an ongoing decision process, not simply a rate change. It depends on reliable information, clear decision rights, coordinated sales and operations, and an understanding of the property's demand patterns and competitive context.

Demand and booking pace

Review booking windows, segment mix, seasonality, events, cancellations, and current pickup where data is available.

Pricing and inventory

Set considered rate and availability decisions using demand signals, positioning, restrictions, and inventory context.

Forecasting

Document assumptions, compare forecast with actual results, and update plans as information changes.

Distribution and performance

Consider channel contribution and cost alongside occupancy, rate, revenue mix, and operating capacity.

Build a useful review rhythm

  1. Confirm data definitions, access, and reporting ownership.
  2. Review demand, booking pace, rates, segments, and channel contribution.
  3. Agree actions and decision owners across commercial and operations teams.
  4. Compare outcomes with assumptions and adapt the approach over time.

Pricing and forecasting are uncertain and market-dependent. Recommendations should be validated against current property information and do not guarantee occupancy, revenue, or profit outcomes.

Hotel revenue management questions

What does hotel revenue management include?

Depending on scope, it can include demand and booking pattern review, pricing and inventory decisions, forecasting, distribution considerations, and recurring performance discussions.

How often should hotel rates be reviewed?

Review frequency depends on demand volatility, booking pace, property type, lead time, and available information. Teams should agree a review rhythm and decision rules suited to the asset.

Can revenue management guarantee higher hotel revenue?

No. Results depend on demand, competition, pricing choices, inventory, channels, operating capacity, and other factors. Analysis supports decisions but cannot guarantee revenue or occupancy.
