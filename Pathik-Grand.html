    About Pathik Grand

    Pathik Grand is an upcoming 21-room business hotel strategically located in the Talegaon MIDC region of Pune, Maharashtra. Designed for modern business travellers, corporate professionals and short-stay visitors, it delivers a seamless blend of comfort, convenience and efficient hospitality.

    Located within Pune's industrial corridor, Pathik Grand provides easy access to major manufacturing zones, offering corporate executives and technical experts a comfortable, efficient home base as part of The LotusLeaf Collection.

    A Stay Designed Around Business

    Pathik Grand focuses on the practical needs of today's business traveller — from comfortable accommodation and convenient dining to essential guest services.
