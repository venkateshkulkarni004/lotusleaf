Hospitality consulting and feasibility

Make better-informed hospitality investment decisions

Independent thinking starts with clear questions. The LotusLeaf Collection works with owners, investors, and developers to examine hospitality concepts, operating implications, and assumptions before major decisions are locked in.

Discuss a project

What a hotel feasibility review can examine

A feasibility review is a decision-support process, not a promise of future performance. It should test whether a proposed hotel, resort, or serviced-apartment concept is coherent with its location, likely guests, facilities, operating model, and investment objectives. The precise scope depends on the information available and the decisions owners need to make.

Concept and guest fit

Define the guest proposition, facility mix, positioning, and reasons a guest would consider the property.

Demand and competition

Identify demand segments and compare relevant accommodation options using current, project-appropriate research.

Operating assumptions

Consider staffing, utilities, maintenance, distribution, service requirements, and other recurring operating needs.

Scenarios and risks

Test conservative and alternative assumptions, identify key sensitivities, and document what evidence needs validation.

Questions to resolve before committing capital

  • Which guest segments and trip purposes can the concept realistically serve?
  • Does the room mix and facility plan support both the guest promise and practical operations?
  • What assumptions drive the investment case, and what happens if they change?
  • What approvals, infrastructure constraints, or professional reviews need to be confirmed locally?

For projects in Pune and Maharashtra, market, access, infrastructure, and approval assumptions should be checked against current project-specific information. Regulatory, legal, tax, engineering, and environmental matters should be confirmed with qualified professionals and relevant authorities.

Hospitality consulting questions

What is included in hotel feasibility consulting?

A review may examine the proposed concept, demand assumptions, competing accommodation, access, facilities, operating costs, pricing assumptions, and scenarios. Scope and evidence requirements should be agreed for each project.

When should a developer speak with a hospitality consultant?

Early engagement can help owners and developers test concepts and operating implications while plans can still be adjusted. Timing depends on project stage, available information, and decisions to be made.

Does a feasibility study guarantee hotel investment returns?

No. Feasibility work assesses assumptions and scenarios; it cannot guarantee demand, operating performance, approvals, or investment returns. Decisions should also use current market evidence and advice from relevant professional advisers.
