Frequently asked questions

About testimonials and references

What should a hotel owner consider when selecting a hospitality partner?

Review the provider's stated services and relevant experience, clarify scope and responsibilities, ask how performance will be reviewed, and assess fit with the property's goals and operating context.

Can I speak to a past client?

We respect client privacy. If a relevant client has consented to act as a reference, the team can discuss whether an introduction is appropriate for a specific prospective engagement.

How can I share feedback about an engagement?

Contact us by email or phone. We will confirm the wording, attribution, context and intended use with you before any publication.

Our commitment: Accurate representation comes before promotional claims. Testimonials are individual experiences, not guarantees of outcomes for another owner or property.
